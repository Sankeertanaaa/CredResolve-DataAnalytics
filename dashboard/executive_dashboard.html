Decision

The reported 11% month-on-month improvement is not a sustained trend. March is the only full month with approximately +11% recovery versus February. Jan–Jul recovery is effectively flat, while contact rate is slightly lower. Target conversion improves from 3.72% to 4.26%, but the number of first-target accounts falls sharply. The best evidence-backed investment area is better borrower targeting, subject to a controlled pilot because the observed targeting differences are correlational.
